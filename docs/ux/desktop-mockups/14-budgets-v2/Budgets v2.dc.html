14aEnvelope budget (YNAB-style zero-based). Income into on-budget accounts lands in Ready to Assign; each category gets an assigned amount, and Available = carried in + assigned + activity, rolling forward every month. Targets show what's still needed. Selecting an overspent envelope opens the inspector to cover it from Ready to Assign or another envelope. Card spending moves money into the card's payment envelope. The title is the budget switcher.
