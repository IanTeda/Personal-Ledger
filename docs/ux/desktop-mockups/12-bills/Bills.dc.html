DUI-036Bill Schedule (default tab) — per docs/bills.md, a Bill Plan is a recurring definition; the Schedule is the generated, dated rows against it, one per period. Status (Upcoming / Due / Overdue / Paid / Skipped) is derived from today's date, never stored. A ⚑ marks anything feeding Needs Attention: every Overdue row, plus any Due row inside its Bill's own Attention Lead. Past Paid and Skipped rows stay in the same table — it doubles as the Bill History, filterable by status, bill, category/account and range.
