settingsSettings — an index page, then one page at a time

Same seventeen pages, same order and same content as Desktop Settings (package 16), re-hosted in the TUI's one-view shell with the Desktop's Medium navigation (DESKTOP-119, DESKTOP-120). g s lands on the Settings index, a full page with one row per page and a one-line description, under two headings: this device (General, Display, AI connections, Logs, About), which work with no Ledger open, then this ledger (Ledger … History). enter opens a page, which then has the whole View. Its heading is the breadcrumb Settings › <Page>: esc goes back to the index with that page still selected, p opens the page picker in a centred popup, and [ / ] step to the previous and next page without passing through the index. Form pages edit a field in place and save when you press enter. List pages use the same n / e / d keys and forms as their full views. Every page also opens by name: :settings <page>.

window sizesSettings at Medium, Large and X-Large

The TUI picks its Window Size by the Desktop's Breakpoints (ADR-0042), read as cells at 7.5 logical px per column: Medium 96–159 columns (720–1199px), Large 160–213 (1200–1599px), X-Large 214 and over (1600px and over). The 96-column floor is the Desktop's 720px. Height never picks a size. Each card is drawn at its size's reference frame, the Desktop's 960 × 1040, 1280 × 800 and 1920 × 1080 at 7.5 × 15px a cell, so a card is exactly as wide as its Desktop counterpart. Medium is the drill-down drawn above; Large docks the index; X-Large adds a detail pane on list pages.
