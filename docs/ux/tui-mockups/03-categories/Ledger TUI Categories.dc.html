categoriesCategories — hierarchy, income & expenses

Two fixed roots — income and expenses — and below each, a tree of any depth. Kind is inherited from the root rather than set per category, so nothing can be half income. Parents accept postings as well as children, so every node carries two figures: direct, posted to it, and rollup, itself plus its descendants — the detail pane always shows both, because a parent reading 52 400 with no transactions of its own is otherwise unexplainable. Moving a subtree carries its transactions with it; it simply cannot land inside itself or cross into the other root.
