8Inventory: home contents for insurance — items, proof, cover, export
8aInventory (g o). A contents register per home or policy; the title switches (o). The first question is whether you're covered: replacement value against sum insured, with the shortfall in red. Room tabs carry count and value. Each item shows its proof as R·P·S (receipt, photo, serial) — dashed means missing. Items above the policy's single-item limit are flagged until specified. Items link to the purchase transaction; a purchase can be turned into an item from Transactions.
