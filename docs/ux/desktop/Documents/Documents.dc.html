4Documents: one library of proof, linked to transactions, items, accounts and bills
4aDocuments (g f, in LEDGER after Transactions). A document is only useful linked to what it proves: each row shows its links — a transaction, an Inventory item, a loan or card, a bill. Facets by type and tax year; search reads text inside PDFs. Renewal and expiry dates are shown in red, so a policy or passport can't lapse quietly. Files stay on disk beside the ledger; the ledger stores links and metadata.
