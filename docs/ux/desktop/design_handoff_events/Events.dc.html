5Events: notable moments on the timeline, and the balances you expect at them
5aEvents (g e, in LEDGER between Documents and Notifications). A notable moment on the ledger's timeline — a pay rise, a car accident, a wedding — or a balance check: on this date I expect this account to hold this much. Occasions can span dates and carry a spend target; milestones and incidents are a single day. Each event links the transactions and documents that belong to it, by tag or by hand. A balance check that disagrees with the ledger shows ⚑ in red until it's resolved. Events also show inline in Transactions (3c).

3Transactions: events inline
3cEvents inline — the ledger scoped to one account (ANZ Everyday). Events sit between transactions on their date as grey marker rows; a balance check compares the expected figure with the running balance at that point (13 sep: 4,182.55, matches). A transaction linked to an event carries its flag in TAGS. E hides event rows; the events chip filters by kind. See 5a.
