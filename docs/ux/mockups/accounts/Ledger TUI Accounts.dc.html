accountsAccounts — list, new, edit, delete

An account is where money sits, denominated in exactly one unit that is fixed at creation — and the ledger never converts between units, so the list groups by the five fixed types (cash, bank, credit card, investment, loan) and its totals are stated per unit, never as one number. Balance is not a column in the table: it is starting balance plus every transaction, recomputed on read, which is why the starting balance is the one field whose edit warns. Delete carries the same weight — an account with transactions is not removed, its transactions are moved to another account of the same unit first.
